# Ergo Social Moderation Rules

---

## 1. Purpose

The Ergo community exists to support open discussion, collaboration, and development around the Ergo ecosystem. These rules exist to ensure a safe, respectful, and functional environment for all participants.

These rules apply to all official Ergo-managed social spaces, including Discord, Telegram, forums, and bridged or affiliated channels.

---

## 2. Core Community Rules

### 2.1 Respect & Conduct

- Treat all community members with respect and basic human decency.
- Harassment, hate speech, intimidation, threats, or targeted hostility are prohibited.
- If someone asks you to stop a behavior directed at them, you must stop.

### 2.2 No NSFW / NSFL / Gore

- No sexually explicit content, graphic violence, gore, or disturbing material.
- This includes images, videos, text, links, emojis, usernames, or avatars.

### 2.3 No Advertising or Unauthorized Promotion

- No advertising, self-promotion, solicitation, or fundraising without moderator approval.
- This includes referral links, giveaways, services, or unrelated project promotion.

### 2.4 No Scams or Market Manipulation

- Scamming, phishing, impersonation, deceptive behavior, or fraud is zero tolerance.
- Do not engage in price manipulation, coordinated trading narratives, or deceptive financial claims.
- Suspected scams may be escalated and reported where appropriate.

### 2.5 No Spam or Disruptive Behavior

- Spamming, flooding messages, excessive tagging, or low-effort repetition is prohibited.
- Behavior that degrades discussion quality may be moderated even if not explicitly listed.

## 3. Discussion Standards

### 3.1 Good-Faith Participation [NEED EXAMPLES]

- Assume good faith when interpreting others' statements.
- Respond to the strongest reasonable interpretation of what someone says.
- Debate ideas—not people.

### 3.2 Thoughtful & Constructive Contributions [EXAMPLES]

- As topics become more divisive or sensitive, comments should become more thoughtful and substantive, not less.
- Constructive criticism is welcome; hostility is not.

### 3.3 Accuracy & Responsibility

- Do not knowingly spread misinformation or unverified claims.
- Verify information before sharing, especially regarding security, finances, or legal matters.

---

## 4. Prohibited Escalation Behaviors

### 4.1 No Unsubstantiated Accusations

Publicly accusing individuals or groups of: scams, market manipulation, alt-accounting, criminal or malicious intent without verifiable evidence is prohibited.

- Such concerns must be raised privately with moderators.
- This rule applies equally to all members, regardless of role or status.
- Repeated or reckless accusations may result in moderation action.

### 4.2 No Guilt by Association

Members may not be targeted, labeled, or penalized solely for:

- their associations
- private conversations
- perceived alignment with others

Interaction does not imply endorsement.

### 4.3 No Public Grievance Spirals

- Repeated airing of personal grievances, accusations, or emotional conflicts in public channels is prohibited.
- Moderators may redirect discussions to: private messages, mediated conversations, cooling-off periods
- Continued public escalation after redirection may result in muting or restriction.

### 4.4 Malicious or Bad-Faith Participation

Behavior intended to provoke, destabilize, intimidate, or exhaust the community is prohibited.

This includes:

- trolling or harassment
- racist or dehumanizing language
- threats or intimidation
- alt abuse or impersonation
- deliberate narrative manipulation
- sustained attention-seeking disruption

**Zero tolerance.** No elevation of known bad-faith actors into positions of authority.

## 6. Channel & Platform Use

### 6.1 Use the Correct Channels

- Post content in appropriate channels.
- Do not request moderation action or support in unrelated channels.
- Admins will assign required roles where indicated (e.g., role-gated channels).

### 6.2 Follow All Terms of Service

You must comply with:

- Ergo's Terms of Service
- Discord's Terms of Service
- Any applicable platform or legal requirements

## 7. Moderation & Enforcement

### 7.1 Moderator Authority

Moderators may take action to:

- remove or edit content
- issue warnings
- temporarily mute or restrict users
- remove roles or permissions
- permanently ban users
- clean up or lock threads

Actions may be taken with or without prior warning, depending on severity.

### 7.2 Authority Does Not Confer Immunity

Founders, developers, administrators, and moderators are subject to the same rules as all members.

- Abuse of authority, intimidation, or public targeting is prohibited.
- Admin and moderation roles are revocable for cause.
- Repeated destabilizing behavior may result in temporary role limitation.

### 7.3 De-Escalation Overrides

During periods of acute escalation, moderators may temporarily restrict participation—including from high-profile or authority figures—to preserve community stability.

- This is preventative, not punitive.
- Applies regardless of role or tenure.
- Actions need to be logged in public record (perhaps scraper can summarize idk).

### 7.4 No Moderation Evasion

- Evading moderation via alts, proxies, intermediaries, or ban circumvention is prohibited.
- Attempting to act through others to bypass moderation decisions may result in permanent removal.

### 7.5 Challenging Moderation Decisions

- Do not argue moderation decisions publicly.
- You may politely request clarification in private.
- If escalation is needed, contact senior members or team@sigmanauts.com.

Public call-outs or drama related to moderation will result in further action.

### 7.7 Administrator Accountability & Removal

Administrators and moderators are held to the same rules as all members and are subject to additional standards due to their authority.

**Admins may be disciplined or removed for:**

- rule violations
- abuse of authority
- bad-faith participation
- enabling or protecting malicious actors
- repeated destabilizing behavior
- refusal to comply with collective admin decisions

**Removal Process:**

- Any admin may raise concerns about another admin to the admin team.
- An admin may be temporarily suspended immediately in cases of: active disruption, harassment, threats, severe community harm
- Permanent removal requires: review by the admin team, majority agreement (excluding the subject of review)
- No role is exempt due to tenure, contribution, or founder status.

**Emergency Authority:**

- In acute situations, senior admins may act immediately to remove permissions to protect the community, with post-hoc review required.

## 8. Common Sense Clause [CANNOT BE USED REPEATEDLY OR NO LONGER ADMIN]

Not every situation can be explicitly documented.

- If behavior is disruptive, harmful, or clearly against the spirit of the community, moderators may act.
- If a moderator tells you to stop doing something, stop immediately.
- If unsure whether something is allowed, ask a moderator before posting.

---

## 9. Moderator Discretion & Exceptions

Moderators reserve the right to:

- apply rules contextually
- make judgment calls in ambiguous situations
- make exceptions when necessary to protect the safety, integrity, or well-being of the community

---

## 10. Final Note

Moderation exists to protect the community as a whole, not to determine who is right in interpersonal conflicts.

**Members acting in good faith, engaging respectfully, and following these rules are always welcome here.**
