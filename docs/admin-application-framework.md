# Sigmanauts Admin / Moderator Application Framework

---

## 1. Baseline Prerequisites (Hard Requirements)

These are non-negotiable. If someone doesn't meet these, they shouldn't proceed.

### 1.1 Community Standing

Applicants must:

- Be a current Sigmanauts member [CAN BEEN SEEN AS BIAS, HOW TO EXPAND IN 2026?]
- Have a minimum 6–12 months of active participation in the Ergo ecosystem (Discord, Telegram, GitHub, Reddit, Twitter forums, governance, or community initiatives)
- Be known by multiple existing members, not just one admin

**Rationale:** prevents drive-by authority grabs and social engineering.

---

### 1.2 Conduct History

Applicants must:

- Have no unresolved moderation issues
- Have no pattern of harassment, threats, racism, or bad-faith behavior
- Not be currently involved in an active dispute with moderators or admins

Past mistakes are not automatically disqualifying — unresolved or denied accountability is.

---

### 1.3 Alignment & Availability

Applicants must:

- Explicitly agree to the Community Rules and Admin Charter
- Acknowledge that admin authority is service, not power
- Have sufficient time availability to:
  - respond calmly
  - participate in admin discussions
  - disengage when emotionally compromised

---

## 2. Disqualifying Factors (Automatic or Near-Automatic)

These are red flags that should stop or heavily pause an application.

### 2.1 Behavior-Based Disqualifiers

History of:

- alt usage to evade moderation
- harassment or threats
- racial or dehumanizing language
- persistent trolling or provocation

Pattern of:

- public grievance spirals
- personal vendettas
- inability to disengage from conflict

---

### 2.2 Structural Conflicts

Seeking admin status primarily to:

- "fix" a personal dispute
- regain lost status
- counter another individual or faction

Inability to accept:

- collective decisions
- recusal requirements
- being overruled

---

### 2.3 Emotional Risk Indicators

- Black-and-white thinking ("with us or against us")
- Difficulty distinguishing intent from impact
- Repeated escalation when frustrated
- Framing moderation as "winning" or "losing"

These don't make someone bad — they make them unsafe in authority.

---

## 3. Trial Period

Before full admin status:

- 30–60 day probationary period
- Limited permissions
- Mentorship by an existing admin
