# Sigmanauts Admin Charter

> *Companion document to the Rules — not a replacement*
>
> *This is the moral + procedural spine of admin behavior.*

---

## 1. Purpose

The Sigmanauts Admin Team exists to:

- protect the community's ability to function
- enforce rules consistently and impartially
- prevent capture by bad-faith actors
- preserve trust through process, not personalities

**Admins serve the community first, not individuals, status, or history.**

---

## 2. Scope & Authority

Admins are empowered to:

- enforce community rules
- moderate content and behavior
- de-escalate conflicts
- restrict or remove roles (including other admins)
- act quickly in emergencies

**Authority is collective, not personal.**

---

## 3. Equality of Accountability

- All admins, moderators, founders, and contributors are subject to the same rules.
- No role, tenure, contribution level, or technical importance grants immunity.
- Admin authority exists to reduce harm, not to win arguments.

---

## 4. Collective Decision-Making

- Significant actions (bans, admin removals, structural changes) require admin-team review.
- Admins directly involved in a dispute must recuse themselves.
- Majority agreement governs outcomes.
- [REVIEW BY 3RD PARTY NECESSARY IF REQUESTED]

---

## 5. Emergency Powers

In cases of immediate harm or escalation:

- Admins may temporarily suspend permissions or roles
- Actions must be reviewed by the full admin team after stabilization
- Emergency powers are protective, not punitive

---

## 6. Bad-Faith Zero Tolerance

Admins must never:

- protect known bad-faith actors
- elevate provocateurs into authority
- excuse harassment, threats, or racism for "utility"
- allow chaos to persist for social or political convenience

**Failure to act against bad-faith behavior is itself a breach of duty.**

---

## 7. Professional Conduct

Admins must:

- avoid public arguments
- avoid sarcasm or humiliation
- communicate clearly and proportionally
- prioritize de-escalation over ego
- accept criticism without retaliation

**Admins model the behavior they enforce.**

---

## 8. Removal of Admins

Admins may be removed for:

- rule violations
- abuse of authority
- enabling harmful actors
- repeated destabilization
- refusal to comply with admin decisions

**No admin is irreplaceable.**

---

## 9. Transparency & Trust

- Decisions should be explained at a high level where appropriate [SHOULD BE EVERY TIME, RECORDED]
- Private details remain private
- Trust is maintained through process clarity, not secrecy

---

## 10. Commitment

Admins accept this role knowing:

- it is service, not power
- restraint matters more than authority
- the community outlives any individual
